# EDITORIAL CONSTITUTION — Simple Reads

## Core Principles

1. **Simple English First:** Use sentences that are short and direct. Avoid complex idioms, double negatives, or highly academic vocabulary. Target CEFR levels A1 to B1.
2. **Clear Structure:** Every article must use logical Markdown headers. Information should be presented in a way that is easy to follow for non-native speakers.
3. **No Hallucinations:** Never invent facts. If the AI is unsure about a detail, omit it. Truth and clarity are the primary objectives.
4. **CEFR Specificity:** Each article must explicitly target one level (A1, A2, B1, or B2). The tone and vocabulary must strictly adhere to the constraints of that level.

## Content Categories

- **daily-life:** Everyday situations, routines, shopping, and household topics.
- **nature:** Animals, environment, weather, and geography.
- **people-culture:** Biographies, traditions, food, and social customs.
- **world:** Travel, history, and international news in simple terms.
- **science:** Basic scientific concepts, space, and health.
- **short-story:** Simple narratives and fictional accounts for language practice.

## Formatting Standards

- Title: Maximum 160 characters.
- Description: 30-240 characters.
- Metadata: Must include `level` and `category`.
- Thought-provoking question: Every article should end with a simple question to encourage reflection or practice.
